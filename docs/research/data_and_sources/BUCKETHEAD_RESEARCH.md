# Selective Buckethead research findings

The ledger contains **23 entries: 5 A, 16 B, and 2 C**. Twenty-one entries verify
an artist/distributor date, an advertised tour schedule, or a calendar hypothesis;
two are unresolved research notes. Three 2015 Halloween releases share one
episode ID. The ledger therefore does not treat 23 rows as 23 independent
musical episodes. Sources were chosen without referring to pageview spikes.

The A entries contain four historical advertised tour openings (2016–2019) and
one recurring Halloween calendar hypothesis. The B entries span artist-listed
releases from 2014–2024 and a distributor-listed 2026 collaboration edition.
They do not verify release announcements available before historical issues.

## Specific research distinctions

- [Hold Me Forever](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/hold-me-forever-in-memory-of-my-mom-nancy-york-carroll)
  lists June 25, 2014. Its original release precedes the pageview API's coverage;
  an original-release response is unobservable in this study.
- The 2015 Halloween sequence is represented by its
  [October 1 opening](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/31-days-til-halloween-visitor-from-the-mirror),
  [October 24 installment](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/8-days-til-halloween-flare-up),
  and [October 31 installment](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/happy-halloween-silver-shamrock).
  They are one coordinated episode, not independent replications. Later physical
  versions are not assigned the digital dates.
- [Rain Drops on Christmas](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/rain-drops-on-christmas)
  currently lists December 25, 2015, while the
  [secondary biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckethead) describes
  December 24. The ledger leaves the reconciled date empty, category C. Possible
  platform/edition/time-zone explanations have not been established.
- [Healing Inside Outside Every Side](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/healing-inside-outside-every-side)
  lists April 4, 2020 for the digital edition. That does not establish first
  availability of an earlier limited physical edition.
- [Oven Mitts](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/oven-mitts) and
  [Live Feathers](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/live-feathers) have
  live tracklists and dated release pages. Those are release dates, not verified
  dates when the performances were recorded.
- [Sky Writing](https://bucketheadpikes.bandcamp.com/album/sky-writing) includes
  live tracks as well as its title track. Release counting without edition and
  content distinctions would obscure what musical activity is being measured.
- [Metal Health](https://open.spotify.com/album/06uVHb5vadazfMziLqyljm), credited
  to Bootsy Collins and Buckethead, has a July 24, 2026 streaming-edition date,
  corroborated by [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/game-on-fire-feat-ouiwey-collins/6767608272?i=6767608276).
  Advance announcement and earliest availability across all platforms remain
  unverified. Its final-period outcome has not been examined.
- The [artist website](https://bucketheadpikes.com/index2.html) labels A Sea Of
  Silver as a preview. A preview is not sufficient evidence for a full-release
  date. That entry remains C.

Current Bandcamp release metadata establish a strong artist-reported date for
the listed edition, but can be edited or refer to back-catalog editions. Their
publication history is not an announcement archive. The source catalog records
review date and verification basis; it does not pretend to be historical HTML.

## Predictive experiment feasibility

The current ledger has **zero verified advance-known musical episodes in the
scored development period**. The lone A calendar date there is a recurring
holiday, not an announced release or tour. The predefined six-episode gate fails.
V1 therefore limits the ledger to historical interpretation and source-quality
research. No event coefficient or predictive gain will be manufactured from
retrospectively known B dates.

This does not establish that announcement evidence cannot be found. It identifies
the gap in this bounded research effort: contemporary venue notices, archived
artist pages, or dated announcement newsletters for recent years would be needed.
The older A examples demonstrate the required evidence standard. Expanding such
research requires a pre-model amendment; neither event selection nor dates may
be chosen after observing final spikes.

An article can still investigate how verified musical history changes the
interpretation of this series, while clearly stating that this ledger could not
support an advance-event forecasting test. Explanations remain observational.
