Wikimedia's [November 2025 data-quality incident](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Data_Issues/2026-06-10_Nov_2025_spike_in_bot_traffic)
motivates the predefined sensitivity population; it does not establish
Buckethead-specific contamination. Source verification dates do not establish
that a release announcement was available to a forecaster years earlier.

The artist's edition dates, a podcast directory date, publisher metadata and
contemporary reporting serve different evidential roles. Unresolved timing and
conflicting dates remain disclosed. Packaging preserves the existing external
citations and dated source checks; it makes no claim to a new historical source
investigation.
